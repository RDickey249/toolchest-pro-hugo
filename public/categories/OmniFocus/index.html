OmniFocus

GTD methodology implementation for Mac/iOS with advanced features

OmniFocus revolutionizes personal productivity by providing the most sophisticated implementation of Getting Things Done (GTD) methodology available, combining Apple’s design excellence with powerful organizational features that transform chaotic task lists into clear, actionable systems. This premium productivity platform goes beyond simple task management to provide the comprehensive project organization, context-based filtering, and systematic review processes that enable professionals to maintain clarity and control over complex responsibilities.

Trusted by knowledge workers, executives, and GTD practitioners worldwide including bestselling authors, Fortune 500 executives, and productivity experts, OmniFocus excels at hierarchical project organization, custom perspective creation, and systematic productivity workflows while maintaining the elegant design and reliable performance that Apple users expect. The platform’s strength lies in its ability to scale from simple task lists to complex project management while preserving the clarity and focus that makes productivity sustainable.

Whether you’re an executive managing multiple strategic initiatives, a consultant juggling client projects, or a professional seeking to implement true GTD methodology, OmniFocus provides the comprehensive productivity foundation that transforms overwhelming responsibilities into manageable, actionable systems.
