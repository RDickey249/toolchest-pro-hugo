Crazy Egg is the pioneering visual analytics platform trusted by over 300,000 websites including companies like Microsoft, General Electric, and Airbnb to understand exactly how users interact with their web pages. This innovative tool transforms complex user behavior data into intuitive visual heatmaps that reveal optimization opportunities impossible to discover through traditional analytics alone.

Founded in 2005 by Neil Patel and Hiten Shah, Crazy Egg created the heatmap analytics category and has helped millions of websites improve their conversion rates through visual user behavior insights. Companies like Mozilla, WordPress, and countless e-commerce businesses rely on its click heatmaps, scroll maps, and user session recordings to identify conversion barriers and optimize user experiences.

Marketers and UX designers choose Crazy Egg because it makes complex user behavior data instantly understandable through visual representations. The platform's ability to show exactly where users click, how far they scroll, and where they encounter friction enables data-driven optimization decisions that traditional analytics tools simply cannot provide, leading to significant improvements in conversion rates and user satisfaction.

## Key Features

• **Comprehensive Click Heatmaps** - Visual representation of where users click most on web pages with color-coded intensity mapping
• **Detailed Scroll Maps** - Reveal how far visitors scroll down pages to optimize content placement and page length
• **Overlay Click Reports** - Display exact click percentages on specific page elements for precise optimization insights
• **Advanced A/B Testing** - Validate optimization decisions with statistical confidence and split-test different page variations
• **Confetti Click Analysis** - Break down clicks by traffic source, search terms, and visitor segments for targeted optimization
• **Mobile Heatmap Analytics** - Ensure optimization strategies work effectively across all devices and screen sizes
• **User Session Recordings** - Watch real user sessions to understand behavior patterns and identify specific usability issues
• **Snapshot Comparison Tools** - Compare before and after heatmaps to measure the impact of website changes and optimizations
