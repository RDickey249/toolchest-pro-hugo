When BMW needed to conduct comprehensive customer experience research across multiple touchpoints, they required a survey platform that could handle complex logic and seamlessly integrate with their existing CRM systems. Fortune 500 companies like Johnson & Johnson rely on advanced survey platforms that offer enterprise-grade security, HIPAA compliance, and sophisticated automation workflows for their market research initiatives. Organizations such as Adobe use flexible survey solutions that can be completely customized and white-labeled to match their brand requirements while providing powerful analytics capabilities.

Alchemer (formerly SurveyGizmo) is a powerful and flexible survey platform designed for organizations that need advanced customization and integration capabilities. The platform strikes a perfect balance between ease of use and sophisticated functionality, making it suitable for both survey novices and research professionals who require enterprise-level features without compromising on usability.

The platform's strength lies in its advanced logic capabilities and extensive integration options, offering features like complex branching, piping, quotas, and custom scripting for unique survey behaviors. With support for over 40 question types and complete customization options through CSS and JavaScript, Alchemer empowers organizations to create highly engaging and professional survey experiences that align with their brand identity and research objectives.

## Key Features

**Advanced Survey Logic and Automation**
- Complex branching and skip logic for personalized survey paths
- Piping and custom variables for dynamic question content
- Quota management with automatic survey termination
- Workflow automation triggering actions in external systems

**Extensive Question Types and Customization**
- Over 40 question types including matrix, ranking, and multimedia options
- Complete CSS and JavaScript customization capabilities
- White-labeling options for branded survey experiences
- Custom themes and responsive design templates

**Multi-Channel Data Collection**
- Email campaign management with personalization
- SMS surveys for mobile-first data collection
- Offline data collection through mobile applications
- Social media integration for broader reach

**Enterprise-Grade Security and Compliance**
- HIPAA compliance for healthcare and sensitive data
- SOC 2 Type II certification for data security
- EU data residency options for GDPR compliance
- Role-based access controls and user permissions

**Advanced Analytics and Reporting**
- Real-time dashboard with customizable widgets
- Cross-tabulation analysis and statistical testing
- Sentiment analysis and text analytics capabilities
- Automated report scheduling and distribution

**API and Integration Capabilities**
- Comprehensive RESTful API for custom integrations
- Webhook support for real-time data synchronization
- Pre-built integrations with CRM and marketing platforms
- Salesforce, HubSpot, and Marketo native connectors

**Collaboration and Workflow Management**
- Team collaboration tools with shared workspaces
- Approval workflows for survey review processes
- Version control and change tracking
- Multi-user access with granular permissions

**Professional Services and Support**
- Dedicated customer success managers for enterprise accounts
- Survey design and methodology consulting services
- Custom training programs and certification courses
- 24/7 technical support with guaranteed response times
