## Key Features

• **Dual Product Architecture** - XSplit Broadcaster for advanced streaming and XSplit Gamecaster for simplified game-specific streaming
• **Intuitive Scene Creation** - Drag-and-drop interface for building complex scenes with multiple sources, effects, and transitions
• **Advanced Audio Processing** - Real-time noise removal, audio ducking, and multiple audio track recording for professional sound quality
• **Comprehensive Platform Integration** - Built-in plugins for Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, and other streaming platforms with seamless setup
• **Professional Presentation Tools** - PowerPoint integration and screen annotation features for educational and business content
• **Cloud Scene Management** - Cloud-based scene backup and sharing enables access from multiple computers and team collaboration
• **Performance Optimization** - Efficient resource management ensures smooth streaming during intensive activities like gaming
• **4K Streaming Capabilities** - High-resolution streaming support with advanced video effects and processing for premium content
