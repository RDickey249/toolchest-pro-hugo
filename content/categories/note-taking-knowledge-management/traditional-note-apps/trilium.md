Trilium revolutionizes personal knowledge management by providing a sophisticated self-hosted platform that combines hierarchical tree organization with infinite nesting capabilities, enabling users to build comprehensive knowledge systems that mirror the complexity of human thought while maintaining complete data ownership and privacy. This advanced platform transforms traditional note-taking limitations into flexible, interconnected information architectures that can accommodate any organizational methodology or thinking pattern.

Trusted by knowledge workers, researchers, academics, and professionals worldwide seeking sophisticated information management without external dependencies, Trilium excels at hierarchical organization, note cloning and referencing, and advanced scripting capabilities while maintaining the privacy and control that comes from self-hosted deployment. The platform's strength lies in its ability to handle complex information relationships through multiple organizational paradigms simultaneously.

Whether you're a researcher managing complex academic projects, a knowledge worker building comprehensive reference systems, a professional organizing industry expertise, or anyone seeking advanced personal knowledge management, Trilium provides the information architecture foundation that transforms scattered thoughts and references into sophisticated, interconnected knowledge systems. Its focus on hierarchical organization, advanced features, and complete user control makes it essential for users requiring enterprise-grade knowledge management capabilities with complete privacy and customization freedom.

## Key Features

• **Infinite hierarchical nesting** - Tree structure organization with unlimited depth and complexity
• **Note cloning and referencing** - Place the same note in multiple locations without duplication
• **Advanced attribute system** - Custom metadata, properties, and labels for sophisticated categorization
• **Powerful scripting engine** - Automate workflows and create custom functionality with JavaScript
• **Visual relation mapping** - Graphical representation of connections between notes and concepts
• **Comprehensive search capabilities** - Full-text search with advanced filtering and query capabilities
• **Complete self-hosting control** - Deploy on your infrastructure with full data sovereignty
• **Flexible content types** - Support for text, code, images, and custom content with specialized editors
