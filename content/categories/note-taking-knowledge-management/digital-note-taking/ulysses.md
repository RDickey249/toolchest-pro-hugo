## Key Features

• **Distraction-free writing environment** - Clean, minimal interface with typewriter mode and focus features for immersive writing
• **Intelligent project organization** - Hierarchical folder structure with tags and filters for managing complex writing projects
• **Comprehensive goal tracking** - Set and monitor daily word count targets, deadlines, and productivity metrics
• **Seamless publishing integration** - Direct publishing to WordPress, Medium, and Ghost with formatting preservation
• **Professional style sheets** - Customizable formatting templates for different output formats and publication requirements
• **Advanced writing statistics** - Detailed progress tracking with reading time estimates and productivity analytics
• **Intelligent revision mode** - Track changes, edits, and version history with collaborative review capabilities
• **Multi-format export** - Export to PDF, DOCX, ePub, HTML, and other formats with professional formatting
