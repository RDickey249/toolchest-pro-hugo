This enterprise-grade platform transforms complex business processes into streamlined, automated workflows that integrate seamlessly with existing systems, dramatically reducing manual work while ensuring compliance and auditability across the organization.

Trusted by Fortune 500 companies and global enterprises across industries like healthcare, finance, and manufacturing, K2 excels at complex workflow orchestration, enterprise system integration, and rapid application development while maintaining the scalability and security that large organizations require. The platform's strength lies in its ability to bridge the gap between business requirements and technical implementation, enabling citizen developers to create enterprise-quality solutions with professional oversight.

Whether you're an IT leader accelerating digital transformation, a business analyst automating departmental processes, or a process owner eliminating manual bottlenecks, K2 provides the enterprise automation foundation that scales from simple approvals to complex multi-system orchestrations. Its focus on low-code development, enterprise integration, and governance makes it essential for organizations seeking rapid process automation without sacrificing control or compliance.

## Key Features

• **Visual workflow designer** - Drag-and-drop interface for creating complex business processes without coding
• **Enterprise system integration** - Pre-built connectors for SAP, Salesforce, SharePoint, and 200+ systems
• **Dynamic forms builder** - Create responsive forms with conditional logic and data validation
• **Mobile-first deployment** - Automatically generate mobile apps for workflow participation
• **Advanced analytics** - Real-time process monitoring with performance dashboards and bottleneck identification
• **Governance and compliance** - Role-based security, audit trails, and regulatory compliance features
• **Smart objects** - Reusable business entities that maintain data consistency across processes
• **API management** - RESTful APIs for custom integrations and external system connectivity
