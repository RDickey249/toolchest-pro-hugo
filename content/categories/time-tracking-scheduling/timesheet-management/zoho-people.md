Zoho People revolutionizes workforce management by seamlessly integrating comprehensive HR functionality with sophisticated time tracking capabilities, serving over 30,000 organizations including companies like Emirates NBD, Pernod Ricard, and thousands of growing businesses that need unified employee management. The platform eliminates the costly inefficiencies of maintaining separate HR and time tracking systems, where data synchronization issues and manual processes consume up to 15 hours of administrative time weekly. Instead of juggling multiple tools with conflicting data, HR teams gain a single source of truth that connects timesheet data directly with payroll processing, performance evaluations, and project management.

The modern workplace demands more than basic clock-in functionality, and Zoho People delivers intelligent time tracking that understands context, projects, and organizational hierarchies. Growing companies particularly benefit from the platform's ability to scale from simple attendance tracking to complex multi-location, multi-project time allocation without requiring system migrations or data restructuring. This flexibility has enabled businesses to reduce timesheet processing time by 70% while improving accuracy through automated validation rules and intelligent anomaly detection.

Beyond time tracking, Zoho People creates a complete employee experience ecosystem where workers access self-service portals for timesheet submission, leave requests, and document management. This employee-centric approach reduces HR inquiries by 60% while improving satisfaction through mobile accessibility and transparent communication. The platform's integration with Zoho's broader suite means timesheet data flows seamlessly into project management, invoicing, and financial reporting without manual intervention.

## Key Features

- **Intelligent Timesheet Management**: Advanced time tracking with automatic project allocation, billable hours calculation, and overtime tracking that adapts to complex organizational structures including matrix teams, contractors, and remote workers

- **Unified HR Database**: Complete employee lifecycle management from recruitment through offboarding, with timesheet data integrated into performance reviews, compensation planning, and succession management for comprehensive workforce insights

- **Multi-Level Approval Workflows**: Customizable approval chains for timesheets, leave requests, and expense claims with automatic escalation, delegation during absences, and audit trails that ensure compliance with labor regulations

- **Project Time Analytics**: Real-time project profitability analysis combining timesheet data with project budgets, resource allocation, and milestone tracking to identify scope creep and optimize resource utilization across teams

- **Mobile-First Architecture**: Native iOS and Android applications enable geo-fenced clock-ins, offline time tracking, photo attendance, and complete HR self-service functionality for distributed and field-based teams

- **Automated Compliance Tools**: Built-in labor law compliance for multiple jurisdictions with automatic overtime calculations, break time enforcement, and regulatory reporting that adapts to local requirements without manual configuration

- **Advanced Reporting Dashboard**: Customizable analytics combining time data with HR metrics to reveal productivity patterns, attendance trends, and resource optimization opportunities through interactive visualizations and scheduled reports

- **Seamless Integrations**: Native connections with payroll systems, accounting software, and project management tools plus REST APIs for custom integrations ensure timesheet data flows automatically into downstream processes
