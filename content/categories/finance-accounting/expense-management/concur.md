SAP Concur dominates enterprise expense management as the undisputed market leader, processing over $1.5 trillion in expenses annually for 46,000+ organizations including 70% of the Fortune 500 who rely on its comprehensive platform to control costs and eliminate expense fraud. This powerhouse solution, born from the 2014 acquisition of Concur by SAP for $8.3 billion, has transformed expense management from a paper nightmare into an automated intelligence system that saves enterprises an average of $150,000 annually while reducing processing time by 75%. By integrating travel booking, expense reporting, and invoice processing into a single platform powered by AI and machine learning, Concur catches policy violations before they happen, automates receipt matching with credit card charges, and provides CFOs with real-time visibility into company spending. With mobile apps used by 50 million business travelers, partnerships with every major travel provider, and integration into SAP's broader enterprise ecosystem, Concur has become as essential to corporate finance as email is to communication. Whether you're managing expenses for 100 employees or 100,000, Concur delivers the automation, compliance, and insights that transform T&E management from a cost center into a strategic advantage.

## Key Features

• **Intelligent Expense Automation** - AI-powered receipt scanning with 99% accuracy, automatic expense categorization, and credit card transaction matching eliminates manual entry
• **Integrated Travel Ecosystem** - Book flights, hotels, and cars within policy through partnerships with 200+ travel suppliers while capturing spend data automatically
• **Real-Time Policy Enforcement** - Configurable rules engine flags out-of-policy expenses before submission with automatic routing for exceptions and approvals
• **Mobile-First Experience** - Capture receipts, submit expenses, and approve reports on-the-go with apps processing 70% of all expense reports
• **Concur Invoice Management** - Automate AP processes from PO matching to payment with intelligent capture and workflow routing for vendor invoices
• **Spend Intelligence Dashboard** - Executive analytics revealing spending patterns, policy compliance rates, and savings opportunities with predictive insights
• **Global Compliance Engine** - Automatic VAT reclaim, per diem calculations, and mileage rates for 100+ countries with audit-ready documentation
• **ERP & System Integration** - Pre-built connectors for SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, and 100+ systems with real-time data synchronization
