Sage powers the financial operations of over 3 million businesses worldwide, from ambitious startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, with a comprehensive suite of accounting and business management solutions that have evolved over 40 years of innovation. As one of the world's largest enterprise software companies with $2 billion in annual revenue, Sage has transformed from desktop accounting pioneer to cloud-first platform that processes billions of transactions annually across 23 countries. Their solutions scale seamlessly from Sage 50cloud for small businesses to Sage X3 for multinational corporations, providing the financial backbone for companies in manufacturing, construction, distribution, and professional services. With deep industry expertise built into vertical-specific solutions and AI-powered insights through Sage Copilot, businesses gain not just accounting software but a complete digital transformation platform that automates workflows, ensures compliance, and drives strategic decision-making. Whether you're managing a single entity or orchestrating finances across global operations, Sage delivers the enterprise-grade capabilities, local tax expertise, and scalability that growing businesses need to compete in today's digital economy.

## Key Features

• **Multi-Entity Financial Management** - Consolidate financials across unlimited companies, currencies, and countries with inter-company transactions and elimination entries
• **AI-Powered Business Intelligence** - Sage Copilot delivers predictive analytics, anomaly detection, and automated insights from your financial data in natural language
• **Industry-Specific Solutions** - Pre-configured workflows for manufacturing, construction, distribution, and nonprofits with specialized features like job costing and project accounting
• **Advanced Inventory & Supply Chain** - Multi-warehouse management, lot tracking, serial numbers, and MRP with real-time visibility across the supply chain
• **Integrated HR & Payroll** - Process payroll for thousands of employees with automatic tax updates, benefits administration, and compliance reporting
• **Customizable Workflows** - Visual process designer creates automated approval chains, notifications, and business rules without coding
• **Banking Integration** - Direct feeds from 15,000+ banks with automatic reconciliation, payment processing, and cash flow forecasting
• **Hybrid Cloud Architecture** - Deploy on-premises, in private cloud, or SaaS with seamless data synchronization and offline capabilities
