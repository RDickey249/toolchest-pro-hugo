FreshBooks is the accounting platform trusted by over 30 million freelancers, consultants, and service-based businesses worldwide to simplify financial management through intuitive time tracking, project-based billing, and client-focused features that eliminate the complexity of traditional accounting software. Built specifically for service professionals who bill by time and project, FreshBooks transforms how creative professionals, consultants, and small service businesses handle invoicing, expense tracking, and client relationships without requiring accounting expertise or complex software training.

Founded in 2003 from the frustration of managing freelance finances with generic accounting tools, FreshBooks has become the preferred choice for freelancers, agencies, consultants, and service-based businesses that need accounting software designed for their unique workflow requirements. Creative professionals, independent contractors, and small agencies rely on its time tracking integration, professional invoicing, and client collaboration features to streamline billing processes, improve cash flow, and maintain professional client relationships.

Freelancers and service professionals choose FreshBooks when they need accounting software that understands time-based billing and project work rather than inventory or complex business operations. The platform's intuitive design, project-based organization, and client-focused features make it ideal for professionals who want to spend time on billable work rather than wrestling with complicated accounting software designed for traditional businesses.

## Key Features

• **Integrated Time Tracking** - Project-based timers with automatic billing calculations and billable hour management for accurate invoicing
• **Professional Invoice Creation** - Customizable invoice templates with online payment processing and automated follow-up capabilities
• **Project Management Integration** - Organize work by project with budget tracking, profitability analysis, and client collaboration tools
• **Smart Expense Tracking** - Mobile receipt capture with automatic categorization and mileage tracking for complete expense management
• **Client Collaboration Portals** - Dedicated client access for project updates, invoice viewing, and seamless communication
• **Automated Workflow Management** - Recurring invoices, late payment reminders, and automated thank-you messages for streamlined operations
• **Comprehensive Financial Reporting** - Profit & loss statements, expense reports, and tax summaries designed for service-based businesses
• **Mobile Business Management** - Full-featured mobile apps enable business management and time tracking from anywhere
