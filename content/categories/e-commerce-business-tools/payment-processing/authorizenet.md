## Key Features

• **Advanced Fraud Detection Suite** - 13 fraud filters including velocity checking, IP geolocation, CVV verification, and machine learning models trained on 25 years of data
• **Automated Recurring Billing** - Sophisticated subscription management with retry logic, dunning management, and automatic card updater for expired cards
• **Customer Information Manager** - Tokenized card storage with PCI-compliant vault enabling one-click checkouts and customer payment profiles
• **Accept.js Integration** - Secure payment form that reduces PCI scope by tokenizing cards directly from customer browsers
• **Virtual Terminal** - Process phone, mail, and in-person orders through web interface with Level II/III data for B2B transactions
• **Account Updater Service** - Automatically updates expired or replaced card numbers to prevent failed recurring transactions
• **Invoice & Email Payments** - Send professional invoices with payment links and accept payments via customizable hosted payment pages
• **Comprehensive Reporting** - Real-time transaction search, settlement reports, chargeback tracking, and custom report builder with API access
