• **Global online payments** - Send and receive money worldwide using email addresses or mobile numbers with instant processing
• **Comprehensive business solutions** - Accept payments on websites, mobile apps, and in-store with complete merchant tools
• **Industry-leading buyer protection** - Comprehensive transaction security with dispute resolution and purchase protection programs
• **Extensive multi-currency support** - Process payments in 25+ currencies with automatic conversion and competitive exchange rates
• **Full-featured mobile payments** - Complete PayPal mobile app for payments, money management, and account control
• **Professional invoicing tools** - Create and send professional invoices with payment tracking and automated reminders
• **Robust integration APIs** - Comprehensive developer tools for custom payment solutions and e-commerce integration
• **Advanced security features** - Two-factor authentication, encryption, and fraud monitoring for maximum transaction security

## Pros and Cons

### Pros
• Most trusted and universally accepted payment platform with established global recognition and consumer confidence
• Excellent buyer protection programs provide comprehensive security for online purchases and transaction disputes
• Superior global reach with support for 200+ markets and 25+ currencies enabling worldwide commerce
• Comprehensive business solutions from simple payment buttons to advanced e-commerce integrations
• Strong security infrastructure with advanced fraud protection and encryption for safe transactions
• User-friendly interface makes digital payments accessible to users of all technical skill levels

### Cons
• Transaction fees can be higher than some alternative payment processors for high-volume businesses
• Account holds and limitations can occur, potentially impacting cash flow for some business users
• Dispute resolution process may favor buyers over sellers in certain transaction conflicts
• Currency conversion fees may add costs for international transactions and multi-currency operations
• Limited customization options for payment flows compared to specialized payment processors
