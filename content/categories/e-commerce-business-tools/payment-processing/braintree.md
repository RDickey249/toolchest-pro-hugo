## Key Features

• **Seamless PayPal integration** - Unified PayPal and credit card processing with single integration and optimized conversion
• **Advanced developer APIs** - Comprehensive SDKs and APIs with extensive documentation and developer tools
• **Mobile-optimized experiences** - Native mobile payment solutions with Touch ID and in-app purchase optimization
• **Sophisticated marketplace solutions** - Multi-party payment splitting with flexible fee structures and seller management
• **Professional subscription billing** - Recurring payment management with flexible billing cycles and dunning management
• **Customizable Drop-in UI** - Pre-built, customizable payment interfaces that reduce development time
• **Enterprise-grade fraud protection** - Advanced machine learning fraud detection with customizable rules and risk management
• **Global payment support** - Accept payments in 130+ currencies with localized payment methods worldwide
