## Key Features

• **360-Degree Inventory Visibility** - Real-time stock tracking across unlimited locations with lot/serial tracking, expiration monitoring, and automated reorder points
• **Advanced Manufacturing Control** - Complete production planning with work orders, capacity scheduling, resource allocation, and shop floor management
• **Intelligent Purchase Automation** - Smart supplier management with automated RFQs, purchase orders, vendor performance tracking, and cost analysis
• **Dynamic Bill of Materials** - Multi-level BOM management with variant tracking, substitute components, and automated cost rollups
• **Integrated Quality Assurance** - Built-in QC processes with inspection workflows, non-conformance tracking, and supplier quality management
• **Omnichannel Sales Integration** - Unified order management connecting e-commerce, wholesale, retail, and direct sales channels
• **Advanced Production Planning** - Sophisticated scheduling algorithms optimize resource utilization and delivery timelines
• **Comprehensive Financial Integration** - Native accounting connectivity with automated journal entries, cost accounting, and profitability analysis
