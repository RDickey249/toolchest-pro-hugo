NetSuite is the leading cloud ERP platform trusted by over 32,000 organizations worldwide, including major brands like Williams-Sonoma, Shopify, and Land O'Lakes. Owned by Oracle, it provides complete business management in a unified platform that scales from small businesses to global enterprises.

What makes NetSuite powerful is its true unified approach - everything from accounting and inventory to CRM and e-commerce runs on the same platform with real-time data sharing. This eliminates the data silos and integration headaches that plague businesses using multiple systems.

Growing companies choose NetSuite because it scales with their complexity. Mid-market businesses use it to replace multiple systems with one platform, while enterprises rely on its multi-subsidiary capabilities for managing global operations across different currencies and regulatory requirements.

## Key Features

• **Complete ERP Functionality** - Integrated financial management, inventory control, procurement, and operational planning in one platform
• **Advanced CRM Integration** - Sales force automation, customer management, and marketing campaigns with unified customer data
• **Built-in E-commerce Platform** - Native online store with integrated order management, inventory sync, and payment processing
• **Real-Time Business Intelligence** - Customizable dashboards, advanced reporting, and analytics with drill-down capabilities
• **Multi-Subsidiary Management** - Handle multiple legal entities, currencies, tax jurisdictions, and reporting requirements
• **Extensive Customization Platform** - Tailor workflows, create custom fields, and build specialized business processes
• **Cloud-Native Architecture** - Scalable SaaS infrastructure with automatic updates, global accessibility, and enterprise security
• **SuiteApp Marketplace** - Hundreds of pre-built integrations and add-on applications for specialized industry needs
