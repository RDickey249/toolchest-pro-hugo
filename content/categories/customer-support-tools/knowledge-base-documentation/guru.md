• **AI-Powered Knowledge Cards** - Create bite-sized, searchable knowledge units with rich formatting, embedded videos, and dynamic content that updates automatically from source systems
• **Verification Engine** - Automated accuracy tracking assigns subject matter experts, sets review cycles, and flags outdated content before it misleads your team
• **Contextual Browser Extension** - Surface relevant knowledge directly in Slack, Zendesk, Salesforce, Gmail, and 30+ tools without leaving your workflow
• **Intelligent Duplicate Detection** - AI identifies redundant or conflicting information across your knowledge base, ensuring single source of truth
• **Team Analytics Dashboard** - Track knowledge gaps, usage patterns, verification rates, and ROI metrics with detailed insights on content effectiveness
• **Collaborative Authoring Tools** - Real-time editing, comments, suggested edits, and approval workflows maintain quality while encouraging contribution

• **Custom Collections & Boards** - Organize knowledge by team, project, or topic with personalized boards that adapt to individual roles and permissions
