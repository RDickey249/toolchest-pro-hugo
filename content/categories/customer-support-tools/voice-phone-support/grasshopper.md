Grasshopper is the virtual phone system trusted by entrepreneurs, solopreneurs, and small businesses worldwide to project professional communications without the complexity and cost of traditional business phone systems. Built specifically for mobile-first businesses, Grasshopper transforms existing mobile devices into professional business lines while maintaining complete separation between personal and business communications.

Founded in 2003 and acquired by LogMeIn in 2018, Grasshopper has become the preferred choice for growing businesses, freelancers, and remote teams that need professional phone presence without infrastructure investment. Entrepreneurs, consultants, real estate agents, and small service businesses rely on its call forwarding, professional greetings, and mobile app functionality to maintain business communications while working from anywhere.

Entrepreneurs and small business owners choose Grasshopper when they need professional phone capabilities that grow with their business without requiring equipment or complex setup. The platform's mobile-first design, straightforward pricing, and essential business features make it ideal for businesses that want to appear established and professional while maintaining the flexibility and cost-effectiveness that supports growth.

## Key Features

• **Professional Business Numbers** - Vanity numbers, toll-free options, and local numbers that enhance brand recognition and customer trust
• **Intelligent Call Forwarding** - Route calls to mobile devices, landlines, or multiple numbers simultaneously while maintaining business caller ID
• **Professional Greetings and Extensions** - Custom voicemail greetings and multiple extensions for departments or team members
• **Mobile Business App** - Make and receive calls using business number with professional caller ID from any smartphone
• **Business Text Messaging** - Send and receive text messages from dedicated business numbers for comprehensive communication
• **Voicemail Transcription** - Automatic transcription of voicemails delivered via email for quick message review
• **Call Management Features** - Do not disturb modes, call screening, and voicemail-to-email for complete communication control
• **No Equipment Requirements** - Works with existing phones eliminating need for hardware investment or technical setup
