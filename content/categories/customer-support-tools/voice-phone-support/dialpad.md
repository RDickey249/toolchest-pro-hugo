Dialpad is the AI-powered business communications platform trusted by companies like Netflix, Motorola, and over 25,000 organizations worldwide to transform customer conversations through real-time voice intelligence and unified communications technology that turns every call into actionable insights. Built on Google Cloud infrastructure with proprietary Voice Intelligence technology, Dialpad delivers enterprise-grade communications that go far beyond traditional phone systems by providing live coaching, sentiment analysis, and automated call summaries that enhance both agent performance and customer satisfaction.

Founded in 2011 by the team behind Google Voice and backed by leading investors including Andreessen Horowitz and GV, Dialpad has become the preferred choice for modern businesses that want communications technology that actually improves business outcomes. Sales teams, customer support organizations, and remote-first companies rely on its AI-powered insights, seamless integrations, and unified platform to boost productivity, improve customer relationships, and make data-driven decisions about their communication strategies.

Modern businesses and forward-thinking teams choose Dialpad when they need communications technology that provides intelligence and insights rather than just connectivity. The platform's AI-powered coaching, real-time transcription, and comprehensive analytics make it ideal for organizations that understand communication data is business intelligence that can drive revenue growth and operational excellence.

## Key Features

• **Real-Time Voice Intelligence** - Live transcription, sentiment analysis, and AI-powered coaching insights during conversations for enhanced performance
• **Unified Communications Platform** - Voice, video, messaging, and meetings in a single application eliminating tool fragmentation
• **AI-Powered Call Summaries** - Automatic post-call summaries with action items, keywords, and conversation highlights for improved follow-up
• **Live Agent Assist** - Real-time cards displaying relevant customer information, scripts, and recommendations during active calls
• **Advanced Call Center Features** - Skills-based routing, IVR, call queuing, and workforce management for enterprise support operations
• **Native Business Integrations** - Seamless connectivity with Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft 365, and Google Workspace for workflow automation
• **Comprehensive Analytics Dashboard** - Talk-time analysis, sentiment tracking, and performance insights with AI-powered improvement recommendations
• **Cloud-Native Architecture** - No hardware requirements with enterprise security, reliability, and global scalability on Google Cloud Platform
