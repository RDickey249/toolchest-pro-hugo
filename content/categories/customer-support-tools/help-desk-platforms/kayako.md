Kayako is the unified customer service platform trusted by companies like Canon, Mazda, and thousands of growing businesses to create seamless, context-rich customer experiences across every communication channel. This innovative platform revolutionizes customer support by weaving together conversations from email, social media, live chat, and phone calls into a single, continuous thread that preserves the complete customer journey.

Founded with the vision of humanizing customer service, Kayako has helped organizations worldwide transform their support operations from fragmented interactions into cohesive, personalized experiences. Companies choose Kayako when they want to move beyond traditional ticketing systems to create meaningful customer relationships through complete conversation context and intelligent automation.

Support teams select Kayako because it eliminates the frustration of context switching and incomplete customer histories. The platform's unique customer journey visualization and omnichannel approach enable agents to provide personalized, efficient support that builds customer loyalty and drives business growth through exceptional service experiences.

## Key Features

• **Unified Customer Journey Visualization** - Complete timeline view of all customer interactions across channels with rich context and history
• **Omnichannel Conversation Management** - Seamless integration of email, social media, live chat, and phone calls in unified threads
• **Advanced Automation and SLA Management** - Intelligent triggers, macros, and service level monitoring to maintain quality at scale
• **Real-Time Live Chat and Cobrowsing** - Interactive customer assistance with screen sharing and guided navigation capabilities
• **Comprehensive Knowledge Base Tools** - Self-service portal with advanced search, article management, and customer feedback systems
• **Enhanced Team Collaboration** - Internal notes, team conversations, real-time presence indicators, and workload distribution
• **Mobile-First Design** - Native mobile apps and responsive interface for consistent service delivery from any device
• **Extensive Integration Ecosystem** - RESTful API, webhooks, and pre-built integrations with popular business tools and CRM systems

## Pros and Cons

### Pros
• Unique customer journey visualization provides unmatched context for personalized support
• Intuitive interface requires minimal training for new team members
• Strong omnichannel capabilities eliminate customer frustration from repeated explanations
• Flexible automation features scale support operations without sacrificing quality
• Excellent mobile experience enables support teams to work effectively from anywhere

### Cons
• Higher pricing compared to basic help desk solutions may limit adoption for very small teams
• Advanced customization options may require technical expertise to implement fully
• Learning curve for teams transitioning from traditional ticketing systems
• Some enterprise features may be limited compared to larger, more complex platforms
• Integration setup can be complex for organizations with many existing tools
