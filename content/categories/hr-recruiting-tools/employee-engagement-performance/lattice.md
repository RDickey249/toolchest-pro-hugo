Lattice is the comprehensive people management platform trusted by over 5,000 companies including Slack, Coinbase, and thousands of high-growth organizations to build exceptional teams through integrated performance management and employee development. Founded by people operations experts, it has become the modern standard for data-driven people management that drives both performance and engagement.

What makes Lattice exceptional is its holistic approach that connects performance reviews, goal tracking, career development, and engagement into one unified platform. This integrated view enables managers to understand the complete employee journey while providing actionable insights that drive both individual growth and organizational success.

People leaders and progressive managers choose Lattice because it transforms fragmented HR processes into a cohesive system that employees actually enjoy using. From startups to enterprises, it provides the people management foundation that high-performing cultures demand.

## Key Features

• **Comprehensive Performance Review System** - Customizable review cycles with 360-degree feedback, calibration tools, and bias reduction features
• **Advanced Goal Management Platform** - OKR tracking, goal alignment, and real-time progress monitoring across all organizational levels
• **Structured Career Development Planning** - Growth frameworks, competency mapping, and personalized development pathways
• **Employee Engagement Analytics** - Pulse surveys, feedback collection, and sentiment analysis with actionable insights
• **Intelligent 1-on-1 Meeting Tools** - Agenda templates, talking points, and conversation tracking for effective manager relationships
• **Advanced People Analytics Dashboard** - Data-driven insights into workforce performance, retention, and development trends
• **Multi-Source Feedback Collection** - 360-degree evaluations with peer, manager, and direct report input
• **Recognition and Growth Tracking** - Peer recognition tools and skill development monitoring
