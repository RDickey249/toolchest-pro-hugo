Accurate Background is the comprehensive screening services platform trusted by thousands of employers across healthcare, education, finance, and government sectors to make informed hiring decisions. With over 30 years of experience in background verification, this established provider offers industry-specific screening packages that ensure compliance while protecting organizations from hiring risks.

Founded in 1997, Accurate Background has built a reputation for thorough, reliable screening services that help organizations maintain safe workplaces. Healthcare systems, school districts, and financial institutions rely on their specialized industry expertise and comprehensive verification processes to meet regulatory requirements and protect vulnerable populations.

HR professionals choose Accurate Background for its combination of technological innovation and human expertise. The platform's industry-specific screening packages, compliance management tools, and dedicated customer support ensure that organizations can navigate complex regulatory requirements while making confident hiring decisions based on complete, accurate information.

## Key Features

• **Comprehensive Criminal Background Checks** - Multi-jurisdictional criminal history searches with courthouse verification and ongoing monitoring
• **Employment and Reference Verification** - Thorough work history confirmation with previous employers and professional references
• **Education and Credential Verification** - Academic degree validation, professional license verification, and certification checks
• **Industry-Specific Screening Packages** - Specialized packages for healthcare, education, finance, transportation, and government sectors
• **Professional Service Support** - Dedicated account management with expert guidance on compliance and screening best practices
• **Regulatory Compliance Management** - FCRA, state-specific, and industry regulation adherence with automatic updates
• **Detailed Reporting and Analytics** - Comprehensive verification results with customizable reports and hiring decision insights
• **Technology Integration** - API integration with major HRIS and ATS platforms for streamlined workflows
