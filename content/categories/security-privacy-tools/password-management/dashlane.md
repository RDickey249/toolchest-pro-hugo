## Key Features

• **Password Health Score** - Real-time security assessment with actionable improvement recommendations
• **Automatic password changer** - One-click password updates for hundreds of supported websites
• **Unlimited VPN protection** - Secure, private browsing without bandwidth restrictions or logs
• **Dark web monitoring** - Continuous scanning and instant alerts when your data appears in breaches
• **Secure file storage** - Encrypted storage for sensitive documents, IDs, and payment information
• **Identity Dashboard** - Comprehensive view of your security status and breach exposure
• **Emergency contacts** - Grant trusted individuals access to your vault in emergencies
• **Identity theft insurance** - Up to $1 million coverage for identity restoration (US only)
