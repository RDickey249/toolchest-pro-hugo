## Key Features

• **Zero-knowledge encryption** - Your data is encrypted locally; even 1Password cannot see your information
• **Universal autofill** - Seamlessly fill passwords, credit cards, and personal information across all platforms
• **Secure document storage** - Safely store sensitive documents, IDs, and files with the same encryption as passwords
• **Family and team sharing** - Collaborate securely with granular access controls and shared vaults
• **Watchtower security alerts** - Proactive monitoring for breached passwords, weak credentials, and security issues
• **Travel Mode** - Temporarily remove sensitive data when crossing borders or traveling
• **Secret Key protection** - Additional layer of security beyond your master password
• **Business integrations** - SSO, SCIM provisioning, and advanced admin controls for enterprises
