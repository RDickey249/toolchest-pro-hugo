## Key Features

• **Multi-platform scheduling** - Seamless posting across Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Pinterest from unified dashboard
• **Visual content calendar** - Comprehensive overview of scheduled content with drag-and-drop rescheduling capabilities
• **Optimal timing intelligence** - AI-powered recommendations for best posting times based on audience engagement data
• **Comprehensive analytics** - Detailed performance insights with engagement tracking, reach analysis, and growth metrics
• **Team collaboration tools** - Approval workflows, content queues, and role-based permissions for organized teamwork
• **Content curation extension** - Browser extension for easy sharing and scheduling of discovered content
• **Mobile app management** - Full-featured mobile apps for content scheduling and monitoring on-the-go
• **Hashtag and mention tools** - Smart hashtag suggestions and mention tracking for enhanced content discoverability
