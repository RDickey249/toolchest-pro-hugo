Soapbox by Wistia is the simple video messaging tool trusted by support teams, sales professionals, and remote workers worldwide to create and share quick video explanations with the same ease as taking a screenshot, transforming how teams communicate by replacing long emails and complex meetings with clear, personal video messages. Built by the video experts at Wistia, Soapbox combines the simplicity of casual video creation with professional-grade hosting and analytics that ensure every video message loads quickly, looks polished, and provides insights into viewer engagement.

Developed by Wistia's team with deep expertise in business video technology, Soapbox has become the preferred choice for customer support teams, sales representatives, and remote professionals who need to explain complex concepts, demonstrate solutions, or provide personalized communication without the overhead of traditional video production tools. Support agents, sales teams, and managers rely on its one-click recording, instant sharing capabilities, and professional hosting to create effective video communications that build stronger relationships and resolve issues faster than text-based alternatives.

Professional teams and customer-facing roles choose Soapbox when they need video communication that prioritizes simplicity and speed over complex production features. The platform's Chrome extension integration, split-screen recording capabilities, and Wistia-powered hosting make it ideal for professionals who understand that effective communication often requires showing rather than telling, but don't have time for complicated video creation workflows.

## Key Features

• **One-Click Chrome Extension Recording** - Instant video creation directly from browser with simple click-to-record functionality
• **Professional Split-Screen Mode** - Simultaneous screen and webcam recording for personal connection while demonstrating content
• **Instant Shareable Links** - Immediate video sharing with secure, trackable links that work across all devices and platforms
• **Smart Video Editing Tools** - Basic trimming, custom thumbnails, and professional presentation features for polished messages
• **Comprehensive Viewer Analytics** - Detailed insights into who watched, viewing duration, and engagement patterns for follow-up optimization
• **Enterprise Security Features** - Password protection, secure hosting, and privacy controls for sensitive business communications
• **Professional Video Hosting** - Wistia-powered infrastructure ensures fast loading, reliable playback, and professional appearance
• **Seamless Workflow Integration** - Easy integration with email, messaging platforms, and business tools for streamlined communication
