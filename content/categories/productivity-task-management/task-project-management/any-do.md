## Key Features

• **Voice task creation** - Add tasks hands-free using natural speech recognition across all devices
• **Daily planning assistant** - AI-powered daily planning that suggests optimal task scheduling
• **Calendar integration** - Unified view combining tasks, appointments, and events in one interface
• **Location-based reminders** - Get notified when you arrive or leave specific locations
• **Shared lists and collaboration** - Coordinate tasks and projects with family, friends, or colleagues
• **Smart categorization** - Automatic organization of tasks into Today, Tomorrow, Upcoming, and Someday
• **Cross-platform sync** - Seamless synchronization across iOS, Android, web, and desktop platforms
• **Customizable templates** - Pre-built templates for common projects, grocery lists, and routines
