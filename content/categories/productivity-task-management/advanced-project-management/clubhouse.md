Shortcut (formerly Clubhouse) revolutionizes software team management by providing streamlined agile development workflows that eliminate the complexity and overhead typically associated with traditional project management tools, focusing exclusively on what software teams actually need to ship great products efficiently. This developer-focused platform transforms chaotic development processes into clear, actionable workflows that keep engineering teams focused on building rather than managing administrative overhead.

Trusted by innovative startups, fast-growing engineering teams, and mid-sized development organizations worldwide, Shortcut excels at intuitive story tracking, streamlined epic management, and iteration planning while maintaining the simplicity that keeps developers engaged with their project management system. The platform's strength lies in its laser focus on software development workflows, eliminating unnecessary features while perfecting the core functionality that engineering teams use daily.

Whether you're a product manager coordinating feature development, an engineering lead planning sprints, or a development team seeking clear visibility into project progress, Shortcut provides the focused project management foundation that accelerates software delivery without bureaucratic overhead. Its focus on development-specific workflows, team velocity, and shipping cadence makes it essential for software teams prioritizing speed and simplicity over feature bloat.

## Key Features

• **Developer-focused workflows** - Story tracking and epic management designed specifically for software development
• **Intuitive iteration planning** - Simple sprint planning with automatic velocity tracking and capacity management
• **GitHub/GitLab integration** - Seamless connection between code commits and story progress
• **Advanced filtering** - Powerful search and filtering capabilities for large backlogs and complex projects
• **Team velocity tracking** - Automatic calculation of team performance and delivery predictions
• **Customizable boards** - Flexible project views that adapt to different team workflows and preferences
• **Slack integration** - Real-time notifications and updates directly in team communication channels
• **Story estimation** - Built-in pointing system with team estimation workflows
