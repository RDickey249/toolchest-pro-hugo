Targetprocess revolutionizes enterprise agile management by providing a sophisticated visual platform specifically designed for large organizations scaling agile methodologies across multiple teams, departments, and business units. This comprehensive solution transforms the complexity of coordinating hundreds or thousands of team members into clear, manageable visual workflows that maintain strategic alignment while enabling autonomous team operation at scale.

Trusted by global enterprises like Samsung, BMW, MetLife, and major financial institutions, Targetprocess excels at complex dependency tracking, multi-team coordination, and executive visibility while maintaining the flexibility that allows organizations to implement their unique agile frameworks. The platform's strength lies in its ability to provide enterprise-level oversight without micromanaging individual teams, creating the perfect balance between autonomy and alignment.

Whether you're an agile coach scaling frameworks across an enterprise, a program manager coordinating multiple product lines, or an executive seeking visibility into large-scale transformations, Targetprocess provides the enterprise agile foundation that grows with organizational complexity. Its focus on visual management, scalable architecture, and strategic alignment makes it essential for large organizations executing sophisticated agile transformations.

## Key Features

• **Visual enterprise scaling** - Customizable views for coordinating work across hundreds of teams and projects
• **Flexible work item modeling** - Create custom entities and relationships that match your organizational structure
• **Advanced dependency tracking** - Visualize and manage complex inter-team and cross-project dependencies
• **Portfolio management** - Strategic oversight with OKRs, roadmaps, and executive dashboards
• **Multi-framework support** - Accommodate SAFe, LeSS, Spotify model, and custom agile implementations
• **Enterprise reporting** - Advanced analytics, custom reports, and data visualization capabilities
• **Integration ecosystem** - Connect with development tools, HR systems, and enterprise platforms
• **Governance controls** - Role-based permissions, audit trails, and compliance features
