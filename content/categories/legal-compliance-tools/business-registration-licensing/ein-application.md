While the IRS provides free EIN applications, their limited hours (7am-10pm ET weekdays), technical difficulties, and lack of support leave many businesses frustrated. Compared to hiring a CPA or attorney at $200-500 per hour, online EIN services deliver the same expertise at a fraction of the cost. Where incorporation services like IncFile provide EIN as part of packages, standalone EIN services offer more flexibility and faster processing. Against DIY approaches that risk rejection and delays, professional services ensure your business gets its tax ID right the first time.
