## Key Features

• **Advanced consent management** - Customizable consent banners with granular cookie control and user preference centers
• **Continuous privacy monitoring** - Real-time compliance tracking across websites and applications with automated alerts
• **Comprehensive vendor risk assessment** - Third-party privacy evaluations with risk scoring and management workflows
• **Intelligent compliance automation** - Automated privacy workflows including data mapping and breach response procedures
• **Automatic cookie discovery** - Website scanning that identifies and categorizes all cookies and tracking technologies
• **Professional privacy assessments** - Comprehensive compliance evaluations against GDPR, CCPA, and other regulations
• **Real-time regulatory updates** - Automatic tracking of privacy law changes with impact assessments and guidance
• **Privacy program management** - Complete privacy governance with policies, training, and audit management
