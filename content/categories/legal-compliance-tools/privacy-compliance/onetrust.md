OneTrust dominates the privacy management landscape as the world's leading platform for navigating complex global privacy regulations, trusted by over 10,000 organizations including Fortune 500 companies to automate compliance across GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, and dozens of international privacy frameworks. This comprehensive privacy intelligence platform transforms overwhelming regulatory complexity into manageable, automated workflows that ensure continuous compliance while reducing legal risk and operational overhead.

Built specifically for enterprise-scale privacy programs, OneTrust provides the centralized command center that privacy professionals need to manage data inventories, consent mechanisms, vendor assessments, and regulatory reporting across multiple jurisdictions and business units. From startups preparing for their first privacy audit to multinational corporations managing complex data flows, OneTrust scales to meet any organizational privacy challenge with enterprise-grade security and reliability.

Privacy teams choose OneTrust because it eliminates the manual processes and fragmented tools that create compliance gaps, delivering instead a unified platform that automates privacy workflows, demonstrates regulatory compliance to auditors, and builds consumer trust through transparent data practices.

## Key Features

• **Intelligent privacy assessments** - Automated privacy impact assessments with risk scoring and remediation guidance
• **Universal consent management** - Global consent orchestration across websites, mobile apps, and marketing channels
• **Comprehensive data discovery** - Automated data mapping and inventory across cloud, on-premise, and hybrid environments
• **Real-time compliance monitoring** - Executive dashboards with continuous regulatory compliance tracking and alerting
• **Enterprise vendor management** - Third-party risk assessments with automated questionnaires and contract analysis
• **Advanced cookie compliance** - Website scanning, consent banners, and preference centers with global regulatory alignment
• **Seamless integrations** - Native connections to Salesforce, Microsoft, SAP, and hundreds of business applications
• **Subject rights automation** - Streamlined data subject request processing with workflow automation and response tracking
